2.1 Duties & Governance Rules

Office Bearer Key Responsibilities
President Presides over Executive Council meetings and General Body meetings; supervises all executive activities of the Association.
General Secretary Manages official correspondence, records minutes, maintains membership directory, and oversees day-to-day operations.
Treasurer Manages bank accounts (SBI), receives membership fees, maintains financial records, and submits audited annual statements.
Chief Editor Manages peer-review process, editorial quality, and quarterly publication of the Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP).
