Legacy & Scientific Vision

Established in 1972 at the Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Hyderabad, the Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) is a premier non-profit scientific society dedicated to promoting plant protection research, education, and sustainable crop protection technologies across the nation.

Founded under the visionary leadership of founder-member and first President Dr. K. K. Nirula, the Association operates from the ICAR-National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources (NBPGR) Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad. Completing over 50 years of distinguished service, PPAI publishes the quarterly double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP).
