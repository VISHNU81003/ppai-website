About Us

Society & Heritage

Est. 1972

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was established on 4th December 1972 at Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Hyderabad. Registered under the AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F., Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50).

Founded under the visionary leadership of founder-member and first President Dr. K. K. Nirula, PPAI operates its permanent headquarters from ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.

Regn. S399 (1350 Fasli) ICAR-NBPGR RS HQ

Golden Jubilee Achievements

50+ Years

PPAI celebrated its Golden Jubilee (1972–2022) marking 50+ years of dedicated service in advancing plant protection science, agricultural entomology, plant pathology, nematology, weed science, and biosecurity regulations across India.

The Association connects over 1,500 life members across ICAR institutes, State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), central plant protection agencies, and plant health industries nationwide.

Official Journal (IJPP)

Quarterly

PPAI publishes the double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) quarterly in March, June, September, and December, hosted digitally on the ICAR e-Pubs portal.

IJPP publishes original research on applied plant protection (insect pests, diseases, nematodes, weeds, biopesticides) and is indexed across Indian Citation Index (ICI), Chemical Abstracts, CABI, FAO Agrindex, and UGC-CARE.
