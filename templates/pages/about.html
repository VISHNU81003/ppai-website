About Us

Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) — Est. 1972

1. Society & Heritage

Est. 1972

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was established on 4th December 1972 at CPPTI, Hyderabad. Registered under the AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F., Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50), membership is open to all crop protection scientists.

Founded by founder-member and first President Dr. K. K. Nirula, PPAI operates from ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad under an elected Executive Council & Editorial Board.

🏛️ Regn. S399 (1350 Fasli) 📍 ICAR-NBPGR RS HQ

2. Achievements & Journal

Golden Jubilee

Completing over 50 years of service, PPAI publishes the quarterly Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) (Print ISSN: 0073-6996, Online ISSN: 2584-0681), issuing research in entomology, pathology, nematology, weed science, and IPM.

IJPP is indexed in Indian Citation Index, Bioscience, Chemical Abstracts, Review of Applied Entomology, Review of Plant Pathology, CAB Abstracts, and FAO Agrindex.

Governance & Membership Scope

The Association functions through an Executive Council and Editorial Board elected once in two years. Membership is open to all scientists, postgraduate scholars, and agricultural institutions. PPAI remains committed to sustainable crop security, biosecurity policy advisories, and scientific excellence in plant protection across India.
