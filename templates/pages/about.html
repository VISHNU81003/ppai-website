About Us

Society & Heritage

Est. 1972

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was established on 4th December 1972 at CPPTI, Hyderabad. Registered under the AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F., Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50).

Founded under the leadership of founder-member and first President Dr. K. K. Nirula, PPAI operates from ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.

Regn. S399 (1350 Fasli) ICAR-NBPGR RS HQ

Golden Jubilee Achievements

50+ Years

PPAI has completed over 50 years of national service in advancing crop health, agricultural entomology, plant pathology, nematology, weed science, and biosecurity regulations across India.

The Association connects research institutes, agricultural universities, and plant protection workers to foster sustainable integrated pest management (IPM) adoption.

National Scientific Forum Eco-Friendly IPM

Official Journal (IJPP)

Quarterly

PPAI publishes the double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) quarterly in March, June, September, and December.

IJPP publishes original research on applied plant protection (insect pests, plant diseases, nematodes, weeds, biopesticides) and is indexed in Indian Citation Index, Chemical Abstracts, CABI, and FAO Agrindex.

Print ISSN: 0253-4355 Online ISSN: 2584-0681
