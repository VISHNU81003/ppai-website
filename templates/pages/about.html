Since 1972 • Golden Jubilee Legacy

Plant Protection Association of India

(Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50 under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860) • National Headquarters: ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) is a scientific, non-profit society established on 4th December 1972 at the Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad. Registered under the Andhra Pradesh Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Registration No. S399), PPAI was founded under the visionary leadership of its Founder President, Dr. K. K. Nirula.

Over more than five decades of distinguished national service, the Association has grown into India's premier multidisciplinary forum for plant health researchers. Celebrating its landmark Golden Jubilee (1972–2022), PPAI unites over 1,900 life member agricultural scientists, academicians, and extension specialists across ICAR institutes, State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), and plant health organizations throughout India.

PPAI is dedicated to advancing plant protection science through eco-friendly Integrated Pest Management (IPM), strengthening biosecurity regulations, organizing national symposia, and mentoring the next generation of agricultural scholars through honors, fellowships, and academic thesis awards.

Indian Journal of Plant Protection

The official double-blind peer-reviewed quarterly journal of PPAI (Print ISSN: 0253-4355), published regularly in March, June, September, and December. Digitally hosted on the official ICAR ePubs portal and indexed in the UGC-CARE List, CABI Abstracts, Indian Citation Index (ICI), Chemical Abstracts, and FAO Agrindex.

Scientific Mandate & Objectives

Advancing sustainable and bio-intensive Integrated Pest Management (IPM).
Disseminating peer-reviewed agricultural research across entomology and pathology.
Convening national conferences and technical policy consultations.
Recognizing scientific leadership through Fellow PPAI awards and scholar thesis grants.
