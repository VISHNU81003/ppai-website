1.0 Background & History

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was established in 1972 at the Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Hyderabad. It is registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 with Registration No. S399 of 1949-50.

PPAI serves as a premier scientific forum bringing together researchers, academicians, extension professionals, students, and industry experts in agricultural entomology, plant pathology, nematology, weed science, and integrated pest management (IPM).

1.2 Objectives & Mission

The Association is dedicated to advancing crop protection science to ensure national food security and sustainable agriculture.

# Core Objective
1 Promote scientific research and development in all disciplines of plant protection.
2 Publish the quarterly double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP).
3 Organize national and international symposia, conferences, workshops, and seminars.
4 Recognize outstanding contributions of scientists through prestigious awards and honors.
5 Foster collaboration among research institutes, agricultural universities, and plant protection industries.
