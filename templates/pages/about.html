About PPAI

Promoting science, research, and application in plant protection since 1972.

1.0 Background & Registration

Established in 1972 at CPPTI Hyderabad, PPAI is registered under the AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50). It operates from ICAR-NBPGR RS, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.

Society Name Plant Protection Association of India
Year Est. 1972 (54 Years of Service)
Headquarters ICAR-NBPGR RS, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad
Official Journal Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP)
ISSN Print: 0073-6996 | Online: 2584-0681

1.2 Core Aims & Objectives

# Objective Statement
1 Promote research & education in plant protection across India.
2 Publish quarterly peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection.
3 Organize symposia, seminars, and lectures for plant protection workers.
4 Confer prestigious awards for outstanding contributions to crop science.
5 Facilitate national and international scientific collaboration.
