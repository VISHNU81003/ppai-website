About Us

Society Foundation & History

Established 1972

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was founded on 4th December 1972 at Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad. Registered under AP Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Registration No. S399 of 1949-50).

Established under Founder President Dr. K. K. Nirula, PPAI operates its permanent national headquarters from ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.

✓ Registered Under Societies Registration Act XXI (Registration No. S399)
✓ Permanent Headquarters at ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Hyderabad

Golden Jubilee & Achievements

50+ Years Milestone

PPAI celebrated its Golden Jubilee (1972–2022) representing over 50 years of distinguished national service advancing crop health, entomology, pathology, nematology, weed science, and biosecurity regulations.

The Association unites over 1,500 life member scientists, researchers, and academic leaders across ICAR institutes, State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), and plant health organizations across India.

✓ Uniting Over 1,500 Life Member Agricultural Scientists Nationwide
✓ Promoting Eco-Friendly Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Adoption

Official Journal (IJPP)

Quarterly Publication

PPAI publishes the double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) quarterly in March, June, September, and December (Print ISSN: 0253-4355), hosted digitally on the official ICAR e-Pubs portal.

IJPP publishes original research on applied plant protection and is indexed across Indian Citation Index (ICI), Chemical Abstracts, CABI Abstracts, FAO Agrindex, and UGC-CARE List.

✓ Official Print ISSN 0253-4355 & Digital Hosting on ICAR e-Pubs Portal
✓ Indexed Across ICI, Chemical Abstracts, CABI, FAO Agrindex & UGC-CARE
