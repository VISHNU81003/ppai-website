Since 1972 • 50+ Years Legacy

Plant Protection Association of India

A scientific, non-profit society established in 1972, dedicated to advancing plant health, sustainable crop biosecurity, and agricultural science nationwide.

(Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50 under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860) • National Headquarters: ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad

01 / Foundation & History

Genesis & Founding Leadership

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was founded on 4th December 1972 at the Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad. Registered under the Andhra Pradesh Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Registration No. S399), the Association was established under the leadership of Founder President Dr. K. K. Nirula alongside visionary agricultural scientists.

Headquartered permanently at the ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad, PPAI operates as an autonomous national body collaborating with government, academic, and research organizations to safeguard India's agricultural ecosystem.

02 / Golden Jubilee & Community

Over 50 Years of National Scientific Service

Celebrating its landmark Golden Jubilee (1972–2022), PPAI represents more than fifty years of distinguished service to Indian agriculture. Today, the Association unites over 1,900 life member agricultural scientists, academic researchers, and plant protection specialists across ICAR institutes, State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), and premier research institutions nationwide.

The society serves as a multidisciplinary platform encompassing Entomology, Plant Pathology, Nematology, Weed Science, Plant Biosecurity, and Agricultural Chemistry, fostering collaborative research and eco-friendly crop protection packages.

03 / Official Publication

Indian Journal of Plant Protection

PPAI publishes the premier double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) quarterly in March, June, September, and December (Print ISSN: 0253-4355). Hosted digitally on the official ICAR ePubs portal, IJPP provides global open-access dissemination for original research, scientific reviews, and critical findings in plant health.

The journal is indexed in prestigious global and national databases including the UGC-CARE List, CABI Abstracts, Indian Citation Index (ICI), Chemical Abstracts, and FAO Agrindex.

04 / Scientific Mandate

Core Objectives & Mission

Guided by its founding mandate, the Association focuses on key strategic goals to advance plant protection science and food security:

Integrated Pest Management: Promoting bio-intensive, eco-friendly crop protection practices to reduce pesticide reliance.
Scientific Dissemination: Publishing quarterly IJPP issues, technical bulletins, and conference proceedings.
National Symposia: Convening biennial national conferences, policy workshops, and scientist-farmer dialogues.
Academic Recognition: Honoring distinguished scientists with Fellow PPAI awards and sponsoring Best M.Sc./Ph.D. Thesis awards for young researchers.
