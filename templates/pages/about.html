About Us

Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI)

Society Overview & Legacy

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) is a premier scientific society established on 4th December 1972 at the Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Hyderabad. Registered under the Andhra Pradesh (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F., Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50), membership is open to all scientists, researchers, and professionals dedicated to the cause of plant protection.

Founded under the stewardship of founder-member and first President Dr. K. K. Nirula, the Association operates from the ICAR-National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources (NBPGR) Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad. Having completed over 50 years of service, PPAI publishes the double-blind peer-reviewed quarterly Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) (Print ISSN: 0073-6996, Online ISSN: 2584-0681), indexed across Indian Citation Index, Bioscience, Chemical Abstracts, Review of Applied Entomology, Review of Plant Pathology, CAB Abstracts, and FAO Agrindex.

Core Aims & Objectives

1

Research & Education

Advance the cause of plant protection by encouraging and promoting research and education in the country.

2

Journal Publication

Provide a national scientific forum for dissemination of crop protection research through IJPP.

3

Symposia & Seminars

Organize lectures, discussions, seminars, and symposia on emerging crop protection challenges.

4

Plant Protection Network

Maintain a link between plant protection workers, universities, and research institutes across India.

5

Global Exchange

Facilitate closer association among members and scientific workers in India and abroad.

6

IPM Technology Adoption

Involve in activities useful for the advancement and adoption of integrated plant protection.
