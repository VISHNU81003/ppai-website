About Us

🏛️ Society & Heritage

Est. 1972

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was established on 4th December 1972 at CPPTI, Hyderabad. Registered under the AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F., Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50).

Founded by founder-member and first President Dr. K. K. Nirula, PPAI operates from ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.

Regn. S399 (1350 Fasli) ICAR-NBPGR RS HQ

🏆 Golden Jubilee Achievements

50+ Years

PPAI has completed over 50 years of distinguished service in advancing crop health, agricultural entomology, plant pathology, nematology, weed science, and biosecurity regulations across India.

The Association bridges research institutes, agricultural universities, and plant protection workers to foster sustainable integrated pest management (IPM).

National Scientific Forum Eco-Friendly IPM

📖 Official Journal (IJPP)

Quarterly

PPAI publishes the quarterly double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) (Print ISSN: 0073-6996, Online ISSN: 2584-0681) issued in March, June, September, and December.

IJPP is indexed across Indian Citation Index, Bioscience, Chemical Abstracts, Review of Applied Entomology, Review of Plant Pathology, CAB Abstracts, and FAO Agrindex.
