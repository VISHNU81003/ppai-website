Genesis & Founding Legacy Established 1972

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) was formally established on December 4, 1972 at the Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad. Registered under the Andhra Pradesh Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Registration No. S399), PPAI has served as the national nexus for plant health practitioners for more than five decades.

Steered by Founder President Dr. K. K. Nirula and eminent visionary entomologists and pathologists, the Association established its permanent national headquarters at the ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad, maintaining strong collaboration across national and international agricultural councils.

✓ Registered Scientific Society under Act XXI of 1860
✓ Permanent Secretarial Base at ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station
02

Golden Jubilee & Scientific Community 50+ Years Milestone

Celebrating its landmark Golden Jubilee (1972–2022), PPAI represents fifty years of dedicated service to Indian agriculture. The Association unites a multidisciplinary community of over 1,500 life member scientists, academic scholars, extension officers, and industry specialists spanning Entomology, Plant Pathology, Nematology, Weed Science, and Agricultural Chemistry.

Through biennial national symposia, policy advisory panels, and collaborative research initiatives with ICAR institutes and State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), PPAI accelerates the adoption of bio-intensive Integrated Pest Management (IPM), minimizing pesticide residue and strengthening crop resilience.

✓ 1,500+ Life Member Agricultural Scientists Across India
✓ Advisory Partnerships with ICAR & State Agricultural Universities
03

The Official Journal: IJPP Print ISSN: 0253-4355

PPAI publishes the premier double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) quarterly in March, June, September, and December. Hosted digitally on the official ICAR e-Pubs Portal, IJPP serves as an authoritative medium for groundbreaking original research, critical reviews, and short communications.

IJPP maintains rigorous international editorial standards and is thoroughly indexed in UGC-CARE List, Indian Citation Index (ICI), Chemical Abstracts, CABI Abstracts, and FAO Agrindex, ensuring widespread global visibility for Indian agricultural discoveries.

✓ Digital Journal Access on Official ICAR e-Pubs Platform
✓ Indexed in UGC-CARE, CABI, ICI, Chemical Abstracts & FAO

Scientific Mandate & Core Objectives 6 Strategic Pillars

The mission of the Plant Protection Association of India is guided by six foundational pillars designed to foster scientific discovery, safeguard biodiversity, and nurture future agricultural researchers:

✓ IPM Advancement: Developing sustainable, eco-friendly crop protection packages
✓ Scientific Dissemination: Publishing quarterly IJPP volumes and technical monographs
✓ National Symposia: Organizing biennial conferences and policy brainstorming summits
✓ Honors & Fellowships: Recognizing lifetime scientific excellence and Fellow PPAI awards
✓ Biosecurity & Quarantine: Advising national agencies on invasive pest prevention
✓ Scholar Mentorship: Sponsoring Best M.Sc./Ph.D. Thesis awards and youth guidance
