Society Overview & Legacy

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) is a scientific society established on 4th December 1972 at CPPTI, Hyderabad. Registered under the AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F., Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50), membership is open to all scientists dedicated to crop protection.

Operated from the ICAR-National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources (NBPGR) Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad, PPAI was co-founded by founder President Dr. K. K. Nirula. With over 50 years of distinguished service, PPAI publishes the double-blind peer-reviewed quarterly Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP).
