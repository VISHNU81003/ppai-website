1.0 Background & Registration Particulars

PPAI is a non-profit scientific organization established in 1972 at CPPTI Hyderabad. Registered under the AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F., Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50).

Society Name Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI)
Est. & Regn. Date 4th December 1972 (Act I of 1350 F.)
Operating Office ICAR-NBPGR RS, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad – 500030
Governance Executive Council & Editorial Board (Elected 2-Year Term)
Official Journal Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) — Quarterly

1.2 Aims & Core Objectives

# Objective Statement
1 Promote research & education in plant protection across India.
2 Publish quarterly peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection.
3 Organize lectures, symposia, and seminars for crop protection scientists.
4 Confer prestigious awards for outstanding research contributions.
5 Facilitate national & international professional collaboration.
6 Advance sustainable adoption of eco-friendly IPM technologies.
