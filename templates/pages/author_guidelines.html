Author Guidelines — Indian Journal of Plant Protection

Submission & Formatting Protocols ICAR e-Pubs Submissions

Scope & Author Eligibility

Active Member Mandate

Publication in IJPP is open to active members of PPAI. Papers on original research in applied plant protection (insects, mites, nematodes, diseases, weeds, and equipment) are considered.

Routine laboratory studies are discouraged; research based on Integrated Pest Management (IPM) trials conducted for a minimum of two seasons receive priority evaluation.

✓ Active PPAI Society Membership Required for All Authors
✓ Minimum 2 Seasons Field Experimentation Weightage for IPM

Formatting & Manuscript Structure

Max 6000 Words

Manuscripts should be typed in double space on standard A-4 size paper in MS Word format (.docx). Articles must not exceed 6000 words.

Mandatory sections: Title, Author Affiliations, Abstract (200-250 words with keywords), Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results & Discussion, and References (Harvard system).

✓ Abstract 200–250 Words with Essential Keywords
✓ Standard Harvard Reference Citation Formatting

Tables, Figures & Nomenclature

High-Res JPG

Tables must be typed on separate sheets without vertical lines. Statistical tests (SE, LSD, CD) should be clearly specified.

Graphs must be prepared in MS Excel. Scientific binomial names of insects, pathogens, and plants must be italicized with author citations on first mention.

✓ Tables Without Vertical Lines & Specified Statistical Tests
✓ Italicized Botanical/Zoological Scientific Nomenclature

Submission & Contact Information

Email / Portal

Submit soft copy manuscripts via email to ijpp1972@yahoo.co.in or through the official ICAR e-Pubs submission portal. Hard copies are not accepted.

All submissions undergo double-blind peer review. Articles originating from Master's or Ph.D. theses must be routed through the guide or advisory board.

✓ Official Editorial Email: ijpp1972@yahoo.co.in
✓ Double-Blind Peer Review & ICAR e-Pubs Digital Indexing
