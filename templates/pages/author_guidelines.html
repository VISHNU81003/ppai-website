Author Guidelines

Instructions and peer-review policies for submitting manuscripts to IJPP.

4.1 Scope & Submission Overview

The Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) is a double-blind peer-reviewed quarterly research journal published by PPAI. It publishes original research articles, short communications, and critical review papers in:

  • Agricultural Entomology & Acarology
  • Plant Pathology & Mycology
  • Nematology & Weed Science
  • Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Biological Control
  • Molecular Plant Protection & Pesticide Safety

Manuscript Formatting Standards

Section Requirement
Title Page Concise title, full names of authors, affiliations, email of corresponding author.
Abstract Structured abstract not exceeding 250 words, summarizing background, methods, key results, and conclusion.
Keywords 4 to 6 relevant keywords (alphabetical order).
Text Formatting MS Word format (.docx), 12pt Times New Roman, 1.5 line spacing, 2.5cm margins all around.
References Follow APA / IJPP reference style (e.g., Sushil, S.N. and Anitha, G. 2024. Journal Name, Vol(Issue): pages).

Submission Portal & Ethics

All manuscripts must be submitted online via the ICAR e-Pubs portal or emailed directly to the Chief Editor.
