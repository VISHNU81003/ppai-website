Manuscript Formatting & Peer-Review Rules

Scope & Manuscript Formatting

IJPP publishes double-blind peer-reviewed research in Entomology, Plant Pathology, Nematology, Weed Science, and IPM.

Section Requirement
Title Page Concise title, full author names, affiliations, & corresponding email.
Abstract Structured abstract up to 250 words with 4–6 keywords.
Formatting MS Word (.docx), 12pt Times New Roman, 1.5 line spacing, 2.5cm margins.

Online Submission Portal

All manuscripts must be submitted online via the official ICAR e-Pubs portal or emailed directly to the Chief Editor.
