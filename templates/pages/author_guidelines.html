Author Guidelines — Indian Journal of Plant Protection

1. Scope & Active Membership Mandate

All authors must maintain active PPAI membership. IJPP considers original research on applied plant protection (insect pests, mites, nematodes, diseases, rodents, weeds, equipment).

Routine laboratory studies are discouraged; Integrated Pest Management (IPM) trials conducted for a minimum of two seasons receive evaluation priority.

✓ Active PPAI Society Membership Required for All Authors
✓ Weightage for IPM Trials Conducted over Minimum 2 Seasons

2. Manuscript Formatting & Text Structure

Typed double-spaced on A-4 size paper in MS Word (.docx), not exceeding 6000 words. Mandatory sections: Title Page, Abstract (200-250 words with keywords), Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results & Discussion.

References must follow the standard Harvard system (e.g. Singh et al., 2002). Master's/Ph.D. thesis manuscripts must be routed through the guide.

✓ Abstract 200–250 Words & Harvard Reference Citation Protocol
✓ Headings: Primary (12pt Bold), Secondary & Tertiary (10pt)

3. Tables, Figures & Scientific Nomenclature

Tables typed on separate sheets without vertical lines. Statistical tests (SE, LSD, CD) explicitly stated. Graphs prepared in MS Excel (83mm/176mm width).

Scientific binomial names italicized with author citation on first mention (e.g. Helicoverpa armigera Hubner). Standard SI units (g/ha, a.i./ha, mm, ml) enforced.

✓ Tables Without Vertical Lines & Specified Statistical Tests
✓ Italicized Scientific Binomial Names & Standard SI Units

4. Submission, Copyright & Peer Review

Softcopy manuscripts emailed to ijpp1972@yahoo.co.in or submitted via ICAR e-Pubs portal. Physical hard copies are not accepted.

All papers undergo double-blind peer review. Copyright resides with PPAI upon acceptance. No processing fee for active society members.

✓ Official Softcopy Email Submission: ijpp1972@yahoo.co.in
✓ Double-Blind Peer Review & ICAR e-Pubs National Indexing
