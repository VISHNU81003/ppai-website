Membership Information

About Membership

Open to All Scientists

Membership of the Plant Protection Association of India is open to all scientists interested in the promotion of plant protection and dedicated to the cause. The Association follows a policy of open membership to scientists and institutions working for the protection of plants.

Applications for membership are to be addressed to the General Secretary, ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad — 500 030, Telangana, India.

✓ Scientists, Research Practitioners & Postgraduate Scholars Welcome
✓ Institutional Subscriptions Also Available for Libraries & Organizations

Categories & Fee Structure

4 Categories
Category Fee Entitlement
Life Fellow Member ₹ 8,000 Lifetime membership, voting rights, journal access & nomination eligibility
Ordinary Member ₹ 1,500 Annual membership for scientists and research practitioners
Student Member ₹ 750 Concessional annual membership for PG & Ph.D. scholars
Institutional Subscriber As Notified Subscription to the Indian Journal of Plant Protection

Application Procedure

5 Steps
1 Download the membership application form from the Membership page of www.ppaiorg.in.
2 Fill in name, designation, institutional address, field of specialisation, e-mail and mobile number.
3 Remit the prescribed fee by NEFT / RTGS to the SBI account or by demand draft payable at Hyderabad.
4 Send the completed form with proof of remittance to the General Secretary, by post or e-mail.
5 On approval, membership number is allotted and entered in the membership directory.
Apply via Email (secretary@ppaiorg.in)
