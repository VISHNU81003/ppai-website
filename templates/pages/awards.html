PPAI Society Awards & Honors

Instituted Society Awards

5 Conferred Awards

The Plant Protection Association of India gives awards to encourage scientists and to promote plant protection research and education in India. The following awards have been instituted and are conferred through the Association:

S.No. Award Title Conferred For Category
1 Dr. R. D. V. J. Prasada Rao Memorial Award Contributions to plant virology and plant quarantine Memorial Award
2 Sri Dodla Raghava Reddy Memorial Award Outstanding contribution in plant protection Memorial Award
3 Dr. D. Bap Reddy Memorial Award Outstanding work in Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Memorial Award
4 Smt. Kavuri Sarada Memorial Award Best paper published in the Indian Journal of Plant Protection Best Paper
5 Fellow of the Plant Protection Association of India (FPPAI) Eminent scientists with distinguished service to plant protection Fellowship
Nominations are scrutinised and decided by the Executive Council of PPAI.
