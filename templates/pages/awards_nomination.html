Nomination Guidelines

7.1 Nomination for Awards & Fellowship

Awards & FPPAI
Who may be nominated Members of the Association and scientists working in the field of plant protection, as per prescribed eligibility.
Who may nominate Members of the Association; proposals may also be submitted by the parent institution of the nominee.
Addressed to The General Secretary, Plant Protection Association of India, ICAR-NBPGR RS, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad – 500 030.
Documents to enclose Completed nomination proforma, bio-data of the nominee, list of publications, and statement of specific contribution.
Call for nominations Notified on the Awards page of www.ppaiorg.in along with the closing date for the year.
Selection Protocol Nominations are scrutinised and decided by the Executive Council; presented at the AGM or conference.
✓ Submit softcopy nominations to General Secretary: ijpp1972@yahoo.co.in

7.2 Nomination for Election to Executive Council

Biennial Election
Frequency of Election The Executive Council and the Editorial Board are elected once in two years.
Who may be nominated Members of the Association in good standing.
Addressed to The General Secretary of the Association.
Last Date Not later than one month before the forthcoming General Body Meeting / Congress.
Circulation The nominations received are circulated electronically to all members of the Association.
Election Procedure The new office bearers are elected by the members present at the General Body Meeting.
✓ Governed by AP Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli
