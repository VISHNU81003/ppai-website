7.1 Nomination Guidelines

Proposer & Seconder Proposed by a Fellow or Life Member of PPAI; seconded by another Life Member.
Eligibility Active Life Membership of PPAI for at least 3 consecutive years.
Documents Nomination proforma, 5-page CV, top 10 peer-reviewed papers with NAAS ratings.
Submission Email Submit PDF to secretary@ppaiorg.in on or before announced deadline.
