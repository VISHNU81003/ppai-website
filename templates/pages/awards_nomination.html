7.1 Nomination Guidelines & Rules

Rule / Guideline Details
Proposer & Seconder Each nomination must be proposed by a Fellow or Life Member of PPAI and seconded by another Life Member.
Membership Requirement Nominees for Fellow and Young Scientist Awards must hold active Life Membership of PPAI for at least 3 consecutive years.
Submission Package Completed nomination proforma, 5-page CV, list of top 10 peer-reviewed papers with NAAS ratings, and proof of age (for Young Scientist award).
Selection Committee Evaluated by an independent 5-member Judging Committee appointed by the Executive Council.
Deadline & Email Submit soft copy (PDF) to ppai1972@gmail.com on or before the announced deadline.
